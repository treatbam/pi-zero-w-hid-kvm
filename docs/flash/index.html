Flash the KVM GEEK (ESP32-S3)

  1. Use Chrome or Edge (desktop or Android). On Android, plug the GEEK in with a USB-C OTG cable/adapter.
  2. Press the button below and pick the serial port. If nothing shows up, unplug, hold BOOT while plugging in, then retry.
  3. After flashing, the GEEK opens a Wi-Fi network KVM-GEEK-setup. Join it, open http://192.168.4.1, and enter the Zero's Wi-Fi, address and console PIN.
This browser can't flash. Use Chrome/Edge (Android Chrome needs a USB OTG cable). Page must be served over HTTPS.

To change settings later: hold BOOT while powering the GEEK on to reopen the setup portal.

Direct download: kvm-geek.bin (flash at offset 0x0).
